## Thu Sep 4 — ✍️ CV · "5 things recruiters look for in the first 10 seconds"

Target groups: general / UK first (post in ONE group today; second group Mon
with a changed hook). Best window 07:00–08:30.

---

Quick reality check: your CV gets maybe 7–10 seconds on the first pass.

Here's what actually gets looked at, roughly in order:

1. Your most recent job title + company — can I instantly tell what you do?
2. Dates — are you working now? How recent is the experience?
3. Job title match — does it look anything like the role I'm hiring for?
4. Where you're based — can you realistically work here?
5. One or two bullets under the newest job — proof you actually did the thing

Nobody reads your summary paragraph first. Nobody counts your skills section.
The top third of page 1 decides whether the rest gets read.

So before worrying about fonts or colours: make your newest job title,
company, dates and location impossible to miss.

What's in your top third right now — job title, or a summary paragraph?

---

Variation for the second group: swap the opening to "I asked myself this after
my 4th rejection in a row: what are they actually looking at?" and close with
"Be honest: does your CV open with a summary? Did it ever get you a reply?"
